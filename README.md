📖 Project Documentation: Bright TV Viewership & Revenue Analysis

📊 Business Performance Overview
Between January and April, Bright TV demonstrated strong monetization and user acquisition across its streaming footprint:

Total Revenue: R 870,780 generated across 19 active channels.

User Base: 10,984 active users with a 91.0% subscription conversion rate (9,995 paid subscribers).

Core Engagement: Average watch time of 8.3 minutes per session, with a notable performance increase on weekends.

🔍 Deep Dive: Understanding the 8.3-Minute Engagement Metric
While an average watch time of 8.3 minutes may appear low for a streaming platform at first glance, a granular analysis of the 10,984 viewership sessions reveals a highly strategic operational insight:

Session-Level Grain: The metric measures individual viewing sessions, not total daily active time per user. This indicates a high-frequency, "snackable" content consumption habit during the week.

The "Home Stayers" Segment: The platform notes that viewership is higher on weekends. This highlights a distinct behavioral split: short, utility-based viewing during weekdays, and longer, lean-back engagement on weekends.
